# KwachaTrack privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

KwachaTrack helps you see where your mobile money goes. It is built so that your financial information never leaves your phone.

## What the app reads

Only SMS messages from mobile money senders (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and Zamtel Kwacha). Every other message is skipped and never stored. If you paste or share a message into the app, it reads that text.

## Where it is stored

In an encrypted database on your phone. The encryption key is protected by Android's Keystore. The app's data is excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

## What is shared

Nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. If you choose to report an unrecognised message or export a CSV file, you decide where it goes using your phone's share menu.

## Your choices

You can turn individual providers off in Settings. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of its data.
